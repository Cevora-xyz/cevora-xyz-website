Bespoke AI and Data Solutions

Bespoke AI and data solutions turn your business data into better operational decisions. They address challenges specific to your organisation, from forecasting demand to allocating stock and understanding financial risk. By connecting models with your existing systems, they reduce manual work and give teams useful recommendations within their everyday workflows. Their value comes from measurable improvements in business performance.

Our standard approach

We work with your team to identify a valuable business problem and assess the available data. We then build and validate a tailored solution, integrating it into your existing systems and workflows. We measure its impact and support your team in operating and improving it over time.

Case studies

Major UK retailer

We built a forecasting tool that accounts for stockouts, matches new products with historical equivalents and recommends purchase quantities. Integrated into the existing buying process, it delivered 60–70% more accurate recommendations for regular items.

Leading US online automotive retailer

We developed software to allocate vehicles across locations based on predicted demand and achievable selling prices. The system recommends transfers when the expected price improvement exceeds transport costs.

Global automotive distributor and retailer

We connected models of future vehicle values and customer purchase behaviour to assess financial exposure in vehicle finance contracts. The solution quantified risk across different market scenarios, supporting financial planning.
