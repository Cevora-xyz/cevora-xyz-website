Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)

Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO) ensures AI platforms such as ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews accurately grasp, suggest and reference your brand, content and expertise.

While traditional SEO focuses on driving website visits from search engine results, GEO responds to how modern consumers interact with information. Potential buyers frequently obtain answers directly from AI tools without clicking through to external pages, a shift known as zero-click search.

Features including AI Overviews, knowledge panels, local listings and conversational assistants are transforming traditional link listings into direct summaries. Within these AI interactions, website visits are increasingly rare. Brands that fail to feature accurately within generative responses risk remaining completely hidden from prospective clients.

Our standard approach

We start by measuring your visibility in AI answers and auditing your website, content and external references. We then fix technical barriers, add structured data and give your content and PR teams clear briefs to strengthen how AI understands and presents your business. Our typical six-month programme includes ongoing measurement and staff training, so your team can sustain the progress.
