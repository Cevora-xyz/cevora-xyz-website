Intelligent Pricing

Intelligent pricing uses AI to set prices based on predicted demand, available capacity and customer behaviour. It combines your business data with external signals to anticipate changes and identify opportunities to improve margins. This helps you respond earlier, use discounts more selectively and make better use of available stock or capacity. Prices reflect the likely commercial outcome of each decision.

Our standard approach

We assess your pricing process and data to identify where better decisions can deliver the greatest value. We build models that forecast demand and recommend prices or discounts within clear business rules, then integrate them into your existing systems. We validate the impact through controlled testing and monitor performance with your team.

Case studies

US vehicle transport provider

We optimised customer prices and driver payments together, using historical data and external factors such as fuel prices and weather. A/B testing showed 4% lower costs, contributing to a 10% increase in company-wide profitability.

Global cybersecurity software company

We developed a model that learns which discounts motivate customers to buy, with strict limits to prevent excessive offers. Discount spending fell by almost 30%, while conversion remained stable.

Major low-cost airline

We built a model to predict passenger no-shows and optimise overbooking, balancing extra revenue against compensation costs and operational risks. The solution increased revenue per seat by 0.5% without lifting costs.
